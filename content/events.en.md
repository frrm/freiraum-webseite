These recurring events are currently on offer:

* **Open meetup (every Thursday from 8 pm)**  
The open meetup is a chance to get to know one another, chat, tinker, and watch cat videos together. ;-) If you have never been to Freiraum, this is a good time to drop by, take a look around at your own pace, and ask questions.

* **CCC Ulm Monday meetup (almost every Monday from 8 pm)**  
    A regular Chaos meetup at [Freiraum](/contact).
    <!-- or at the university in [Cafe Einstein](/contact#uni-ulm).-->

    We chat, order pizza, laugh, and work on computers (not always, but more and more often). If you think you are sufficiently Chaos-compatible, just drop by. Everyone is welcome, and apart from a healthy dose of curiosity, no prior knowledge is expected. Nobody should expect a complete programme to be laid on for them, though. The evening becomes what the people there make of it. We are happy to help and interested in technology, but we are not a support hotline.

* **[Chaos Seminar](/ccc/chaosseminar) (usually on the second Monday of the month from 7 pm)**  
    When there are enough speakers, a Chaos Seminar with a talk is usually held at [Freiraum](/contact) on the second Monday of the month instead of the regular Monday meetup.
    You can find past and planned topics and dates on the [Chaos Seminar page](/ccc/chaosseminar).
    Upcoming dates are also listed in the calendar.

* **Lockpicking meetup (currently irregular)**  
  The Ulm [SSDeV](https://www.ssdev.org) sports group also meets at Freiraum for lockpicking, currently at irregular intervals during a CCC Ulm Monday meetup or an open meetup.
  Everyone who follows the [SSDeV sports regulations](https://blog.ssdev.org/?page_id=281) is welcome.
