### What does Freiraum offer?

  * **Rooms**  
Freiraum provides space for your own projects and for bringing them to life, or simply for meeting and sharing ideas. Feel free to look around:

  * **Equipment**  
Freiraum has a wide range of equipment available (for example, a projector, soldering equipment, an oscilloscope, a sewing machine, and tools). There is also an electronics cabinet stocked with components and many other devices.  
![oszi_small](/images/oszi_small.jpg)
![schrank_small](/images/schrank_small.jpg)

  * **Knowledge and experience**  
Depending on what you already know, you may also find people at Freiraum who can help you with particular problems. Sharing knowledge and learning together are among the basic ideas behind hackerspaces.

  * **Events**  
Events take place at Freiraum from time to time. You can find some recurring events under [Events](/events).

### Anything else?
In principle, Freiraum is open to all ideas and new suggestions — it is a space for possibilities. If you want to bring a project to life and it does not fit the description above, just get in touch.

### Interested?
The best thing is simply to drop by. You can find our address and more information under [Contact](/contact).
