17 November 2011, 7 pm: Microcontroller meetup

Freiraum invites you to a relaxed chat about microcontrollers. Manfred Helzle will introduce the work of the [Microcontroller Working Group](http://www.forschendes-lernen.de/microcontroller/). Among other things, he will present self-designed circuit boards created with educational considerations in mind.
