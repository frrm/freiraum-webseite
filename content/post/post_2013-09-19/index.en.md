For the 2013 federal election, Freiraum is expected to be open on Sunday (September 22) from noon to 8 pm.

We will vote (or not), [create statistics](http://wahldatenhelfer.de/) (or at least try), watch the [Bundesradio election studio](http://socialmediaweek.org/berlin/events/?id=89459) (or whatever else people want), or spend election day in another suitably hackerish way.

Visitors are, as always, very welcome.
