This Thursday, 23 June 2011 at 9 pm, Freiraum is all about lockpicking.

On the occasion of the first meetup of the Ulm Sports Friends of Lock Technology, there will be an introduction to the art of sport lockpicking. After a short talk, practice tools and locks will be available for everyone who would like to try it out.
