On 5 March (the first Tuesday of the month) at 7 pm, the OpenStreetMap group meetup for Neu-Ulm/Ulm will take place again at Freiraum.

The topic of this meetup is JOSM, a Java editor that works across operating systems. It makes it very easy to enter data recorded with a GPS device into OpenStreetMap. The editor offers much more, too. For example, satellite imagery can be added as a background while editing to help with orientation.

You can find more information about the Ulm/Neu-Ulm group [here](http://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Ulm_Neu-Ulm/Stammtisch).
