Since engaging with the social (in this case, political) effects of technology has always been part of German hacker culture, here is a note about the nationwide StopWatchingUs protests, which will also take place in Ulm on Saturday, July 27.

> Rally and march, July 27, 2 pm – Ulm Market Square (behind the town hall)
>
> #StopWatchingUs! The nationwide protests against Prism, Tempora, the NSA, surveillance, and for fair treatment of Edward Snowden begin on Saturday, July 27, 2013!
>
> It is time to make our inner uprising visible on the outside, everywhere. Please invite all your friends, spread the word, and get involved in organizing!

More information and links are available, among other places, [at demonstrare.de](http://demonstrare.de/termine/27-07-stopwatchingus-deutschlandweite-proteste-gegen-prism-und-tempora)
