Hello, hackers!

Shell scripting inspires both love and hate. It is very old and ugly, and does not scale well; at the same time, it is extremely handy for many tasks, so it seems to be here to stay.

In this presentation I will offer advice on writing maintainable and robust shell scripts that handle unexpected error conditions properly. It focuses mainly on bash, but most of it also applies to dash and other shell interpreters.

The presentation will last around one hour, plus time for discussion.

It will be in English. If I tried to do it in German, it would last three hours :)
