Following the general recommendations, we will close for now until 23 April. This decision has been coordinated with Freifunk, amateur radio operators, and OSM.

Our meetups are usually small, but we think we do not lose much by closing for two months. The hygiene situation is not ideal either (no paper towels, cold water). According to the current podcast with Drosten (https://www.ndr.de/nachrichten/info/podcast4684.html), the virus spreads mainly through the air, so people should not gather in large groups in small rooms.

You could argue back and forth that we are all “relatively” young or that there are at most 10 people. My response is that this is not about us, but about vulnerable people whom we could infect outside Freiraum. The main thing now is not to pose a danger to others.

Look after your older relatives/neighbours, be considerate, and do not forget to tinker at home.

See you soon!
