What began last November as a playground for tinkerers exploring audiovisual experiments in the tubes of Sauschdall and the CAT room could become a series. For now, it continues: WOHLFEIL.

For weeks, ideas have been taking shape at Freiraum. On 20 April they will be presented as electronic sounds in the rooms above the university. On two floors, luminous objects drift out of virtual space; in the basement, bodies move to benchmarks and pendulols amid driving Drum'n'Bass/DrumFunk beats; on the entrance level, dance-loving bodies twitch in the techno networks spun from indiTronics, E.B.M. and Wave by tdPaperstreet and lärmLust – Bomb&Crossfloor. In between, there is plenty of room for optical flicker from monitor cascades, interspersed with scrambled playful experiments from the childhood of computer technology.

The game is on!
