Things that belong together are coming together. Since Freiraum and CCC Ulm members know each other well and many are active in both associations, Freiraum now officially represents CCC Ulm as an Erfa. This gives us more support from the national CCC for events such as CPU. Freiraum has also joined the Association of Open Workshops, which gives us additional insurance for our tools and premises.

With the new structure, we hope to offer workshops (such as Roland's locksmith workshop) and talks more often. In recent years Freiraum has become increasingly crowded as we expanded the workshop and added machines (3D printers, laser cutter, milling machine, etc.), so talks often had to contend with a serious lack of space. FreeFM has now given Freiraum the adjoining room directly next door, along with a fair increase in rent. This eases the situation and creates more room to relax.

The new room naturally needs to be well equipped; that is one of our tasks for the coming months. Over the past three months, we have already replaced the wiring for power and network sockets in the old room and tiled and renovated the kitchen. In addition to furnishing the new room, our agenda includes painting the hallway, cleaning the floor, and sanding the old doors — we want all our guests to feel comfortable.
