At the hackers' meetup on Thursday (9 March 2017), we will talk about CIA hacking tools at Hackerspace Ulm (Freiraum). We will look at what has been published so far (almost 8,000 documents) and what may still be coming.

Wikileaks writes:  
“Recently, the CIA lost control of the majority of its hacking arsenal including malware, viruses, trojans, weaponized “zero day” exploits, malware remote control systems and associated documentation. This extraordinary collection, which amounts to more than several hundred million lines of code, gives its possessor the entire hacking capacity of the CIA. ”

We will also decide today on the schedule for upcoming talks (and workshops) this spring. The anticipated topics sound really exciting: brains, consciousness, deep learning, molecular cuisine, and embedded systems reverse engineering.

We start at 8 pm on Thursday at Freiraum (Platzgasse 18, above Radio freeFM).

Welcome!
