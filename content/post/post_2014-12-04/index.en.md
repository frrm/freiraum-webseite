Freifunk is a citizens' initiative that aims to build open, decentralized Wi-Fi networks under community ownership. There have already been some initial attempts in Ulm, but unfortunately the effort has since faded.

A Freifunk meetup will therefore take place at Freiraum next Wednesday. The meetup is intended to bring together people who are interested in building a Freifunk community.

We will start with a short presentation introducing Freifunk. Afterwards, we plan to discuss, make plans, and perhaps tinker. It is due to start at 6:30 pm.

If there is interest, we would be happy to hold this meetup again.

**Edit**: Freifunk has since also received some publicity on Ulm radio station [FreeFM](http://www.freefm.de/) in the form of a [segment](http://www.aau.telebus.de/wissensstrahlung/2014/mp3/Wissensstrahlung_141207_public.mp3) on the “[Wissensstrahlung](http://wissensstrahlung.de)” programme.
