We have revived the Ulm lockpicking group. The plan is to hold monthly lockpicking meetups at Freiraum, alternating between the CCC Monday meetup and the open (Thursday) meetup.

Everyone who follows the [SSDeV sports regulations](https://blog.ssdev.org/?page_id=281) is welcome.

We will get started at the next CCC Monday meetup (June 27 from 8 pm) with an introductory talk (ground rules, SSDeV, an overview of locks, techniques, and so on), followed by some hands-on practice.

Various topics are planned for future meetups, for example:

- Different types of locks:
  - Combination locks
  - "Standard" pin tumbler locks
  - Disc detainer locks (e.g. mailbox locks), lever locks
  - Dimple locks
  - Rotating disc cylinders
  - Tubular locks
  - Bit key locks
  - Safe locks
  - Lever tumbler / Chubb / locker locks
  - "Non-locks" / pseudo-locks
  - Special lock types (e.g. MasterLock One)
  - Possibly TSA locks
  - Possibly electronic locks
- Opening methods:
  - "Standard" picking
  - Impressioning
  - Bump keys
  - Bypassing the lock (shim, comb pick, ...)
  - (Possibly electric picking)
  - ...
- Tools (comparison, quality, DIY)
- Disassembling and repinning locks
- Locking systems
- Design flaws
- Security against lockpicking and burglary
- Locksmiths / opening doors that have closed behind you
