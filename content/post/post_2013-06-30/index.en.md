Here is a brief summary of the decisions made last Thursday:

  * **Association formation**  
We will establish an association for Freiraum to simplify its organization. Anyone interested in becoming a founding member or otherwise helping with the formation is very welcome (just email us or talk to one of the members at Freiraum).

  * **Lightning talks**  
We are introducing a new event series (on the third Thursday of each month). At least one person will give a short talk on any topic. The talks need not be especially polished; the point is to communicate ideas (or problems), not to deliver a perfect presentation. See also [the Wikipedia article](http://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lightning_Talk).

  * **Lockpicking**  
Due to a lack of time, there will initially be no more meetings of the Ulm lockpicking sports group at Freiraum. Anyone who would like to revive the group is very welcome. Some of the equipment can continue to be used if needed.
