There will be another Coders Night on Friday, February 20, 2015.

Coders Night is an evening to work on projects together or on your own — relaxed is the main thing — or simply surf the internet and enjoy the music :P. If you bring your laptop, you should feel right at home.

For a relaxed atmosphere, recordings of [SpaceNight](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CpznCSeqMxw) (known from ARD-alpha) will be shown on the projector. The doors should be open by 7 pm at the latest.
