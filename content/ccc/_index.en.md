Since summer 2018, we have been glad to welcome CCC Ulm as a local group of Hackerspace Ulm e.V. Joining the association allowed CCC Ulm to (re)gain its status as an [Erfa-Kreis](https://www.ccc.de/de/club/erfas), and it is therefore supported by the national [CCC e.V.](https://www.ccc.de/).

A short introduction to the Ulm CCC:

> We are a galactic community of living beings and support educational and public education initiatives relating to new technological developments, as well as art and culture. Chaos Computer Club Ulm sees itself as a forum for the hacker scene, an intermediary between hackers, system operators, and the public.

![CCC Ulm Logo](/images/ccc_ulm_logo.png)


## Monday meetup

The Ulm Chaos community meets every Monday evening! It includes:

- digital travellers,
- Haecksen and hackers,
- Chaotines and Chaoten,
- school pupils,
- students,
- master craftspeople,
- academics,
- and many others

...who usually meet every week for the Chaos meetup on Mondays from around 8 pm at [Freiraum](/contact).

We chat, order pizza, laugh, and work on computers (not always, but more and more often). If you think you are sufficiently Chaos-compatible, just drop by. Everyone is welcome, and apart from a healthy dose of curiosity, no prior knowledge is expected. Nobody should expect a complete programme to be laid on for them, though. The evening becomes what the people there make of it. We are happy to help and interested in technology, but we are not a support hotline.
