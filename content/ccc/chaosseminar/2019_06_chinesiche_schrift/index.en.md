This is an introductory crash course in 汉语/Hanzi and Mandarin, designed to introduce listeners to an unfamiliar writing system. Along the way, we will look at how strokes form characters, how the meanings of characters combine, how children learn the writing system, and how input methods work on electronic devices such as phones and computers.

Please watch [this TED Talk](https://www.ted.com/talks/shaolan_learn_to_read_chinese_with_ease?language=en) before the Chaos Seminar to get a feel for recognizing pictograms and combining characters.
