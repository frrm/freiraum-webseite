Youth protection is a hotly debated topic, especially on the internet, because it often conflicts with freedom of expression and information. Many young people use the internet, but it is difficult to determine whether someone who wants to use a particular service is actually a minor.

The talk presents the current legal framework (the Youth Protection Act and the Interstate Treaty on the Protection of Minors in the Media) and examines the activities of the Commission for the Protection of Minors in the Media (KJM), which serves as the central supervisory body for youth protection and the protection of human dignity online.
