Talk website: http://marmaro.de/docs/chaosseminar/on-performance/

This talk explores ideas about the performance of software and software systems. Examples are used to examine and discuss different aspects of the subject.

The aim is to share experience that helps identify problematic performance optimisations and to provide a more comprehensive understanding of the topic.
