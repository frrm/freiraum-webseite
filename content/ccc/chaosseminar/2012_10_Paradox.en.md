Is light a particle or a wave? Could the greatest athlete of antiquity fail to overtake a tortoise? Can a little demon outwit the second law of thermodynamics? Whatever the answer — or whether there is one at all — thinking about apparent or real contradictions, paradoxes and antinomies has repeatedly led to new insights. This talk picks a few gems from a vast collection.
