This talk is about backup, security, and document management.

Marcus has implemented a setup intended to withstand a house fire, theft, and ransomware. He will present it and open it up for discussion.

### Topics

“Nobody cares about backup, all just care about restore”

- Backup workflow (encryption, copying, photographing, text recognition, restore)
- Attack vectors (fire, theft (laptop, mobile phone, airport security), police raid, Gundremmingen, ransomware)
- Requirements (cloud and costs, requirements for each attack vector)
