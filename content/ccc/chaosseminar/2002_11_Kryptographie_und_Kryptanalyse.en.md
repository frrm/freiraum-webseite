### A brief history

From the Caesar cipher through Vigenère and Enigma to practically secure methods such as DES and RSA, inventive cryptographers have continually developed new methods, only for equally inventive cryptanalysts to break them again and again. This talk describes the methods in sequence, the historical roles they played, and how they can be cracked.
