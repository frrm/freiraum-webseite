The speaker was on a business trip in Nanjing and would like to share a few pictures and stories that may not be obvious to a long-nose — for example, the signs on every escalator telling you to hold on to the handrail.

Topics:

- introduction (what I saw, inferred, and heard)
- political constraints
- foreigners as second-class people
- Chinese people as colleagues and at work
- Chinese people and their phones
- travelling properly (SIM card, bank account, WeChat Pay, and Alipay)
- food
- architecture
- getting around
- translation aids
- China in cultural transition
- selected strange things I saw
