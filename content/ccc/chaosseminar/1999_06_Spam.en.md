### How do I protect myself — and how and where do I complain?

Spam — unsolicited emails or news posts sent to a large number of internet users, usually promising quick riches or lots of naked women — is one of the internet’s biggest nuisances. Although many countries now declare such activity illegal, the amount of spam keeps growing rather than shrinking. We therefore have to help ourselves. This talk explains the technical methods available to fight spam and how and where to make an effective complaint.
