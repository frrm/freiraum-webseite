Today, when people hear “flying eyes,” they do not think of a new game among medical students; they think of drones carrying camera systems. Cameras are now found in cars, phones, (optical) mice, toll-collection gantries, and €70 toy helicopters. And we must not forget the cameras in industry that monitor and help control production. Camera electronics are therefore an ubiquitous part of our lives, and that is what this talk is about.

Camera systems combine a camera, a technical imitation of an eye, with lighting, a processing unit, and interfaces. In particular, the talk compares the eye and the camera, introduces image processing as the associated software discipline, shares anecdotes from more than forty years of camera-system development, and offers a glimpse of what the future may bring.
