The free SQL database PostgreSQL and its ecosystem are introduced.

The talk covers PostgreSQL’s history and current development. Along with an overview, it highlights some technical details and presents various extensions and commercial derivatives.

For anyone interested: the October 2001 Chaos Seminar was a [basic introductory talk on databases](../2001_10_Datenbanken).
