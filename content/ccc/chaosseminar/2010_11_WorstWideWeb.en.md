*The web is broken.*

That is the short and apt summary of this Chaos Seminar talk. Nico uses several examples to show the things you can find on the web that do not work, or that work but do not look “nice” from a hacker’s point of view.

Sites both obscure and well known get gently mocked, and the audience is invited to suggest defects worth demonstrating. Technical background and details will be explained as needed.
