We demonstrate some basic attacks and cryptographic options for defending yourself against them. These include installing Ubuntu with disk encryption, encrypting email with Thunderbird and Enigmail, and anonymizing activity with Tor Browser.

Learn more from [Digitalcourage](https://digitalcourage.de/digitale-selbstverteidigung); for browser tracking, see the Chaos Seminar from [November 2015](../2015_11_Lightning/).

Agenda:

1. Jürgen: Types of attacks (malicious hotel maid, rubber hose, man in the middle)
2. Marcus: Ubuntu installation, notes on password strength
3. Simon: Email encryption (Thunderbird + Enigmail)
4. Veronika: Tor Browser
