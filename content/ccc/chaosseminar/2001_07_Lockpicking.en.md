Skydiving is out and nobody cares about kick scooters anymore; the new extreme sport is lockpicking! Opening locks without damage combines play, fun, and excitement.

The talk covers modern lock cylinders: how they are built, how they work, and why and how they can be opened without the right key. Afterwards, participants can put what they have learned into practice; practice tools and beginner-friendly practice locks will be available.
