Kanban is best known for a board with sticky notes in three columns — *to do*, *doing*, and *done* — but that misses the point: Kanban is a cultural value system for companies, from which recommendations for action, processes, and reporting lines are derived. The speaker has a theoretical foundation but no practical experience.

### Talk topics

- origins
- value system
- comparison with other approaches (Scrum, ITIL)
- representing business processes on a board, swimlanes
- work-in-progress limits
- feedback loops (feedback meetings)
- metrics

Using fictional businesses — a doctor’s office, a car repair shop, and an IT outfit — the talk will explain how Kanban’s values try to distinguish it from the competition.

### Interactive

Please share comments and tell us about your experiences at work with recommended and not-so-recommended practices.
