Ever since modern DSL routers entered the market with various new features such as internet telephony, Voice over IP (VoIP) has become familiar to almost everyone who connects a PC to the internet at home. Anyone who has more to do with VoIP may have discovered Skype and perhaps uses it regularly.

But hardly anyone has explored the concepts beneath the surface. How do you set up your own telephone system? What is the SIP protocol, and how does it work? How is the transition between VoIP and the landline network handled? What about messengers?

The talk first aims to give a general overview of the topic and how it works, in a way that beginners can understand. It is designed from the outset to explore various individual topics in more depth in follow-up talks.
