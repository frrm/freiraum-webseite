The term “hacker” is overloaded with meanings. Ideas range from a malicious data thief to an internet Robin Hood. This talk surveys the roots and eventful history of the hacker movement, sheds light on related terms such as phreaker and cracker, and explains, particularly from the CCC’s perspective, the worldview hackers stand for. It is seasoned with plenty of anecdotes from the “good old days”, when computers were real computers ;-)
