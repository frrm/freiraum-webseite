Firewalls are considered the very embodiment of computer security. So clever marketing strategists thought they could sell one for every computer.

Users are misled about their functionality. Personal firewalls promise security that, by their very nature, they cannot provide. Instead, having a personal firewall creates entirely new ways to attack a system.

The talk explains how personal firewalls work. A few examples then show how to bypass or attack one.
