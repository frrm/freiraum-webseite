Semiconductors are made by joining materials in multiple layers. The more precisely the interfaces between layers can be manufactured, the more accurately the component’s behaviour can be predicted. Atomic Layer Deposition (ALD) is a process that triggers a chemical reaction in a gas and thereby changes the layer. In this particular example, a single layer of aluminium atoms is applied to a workpiece so that the aluminium reacts with oxygen in the next step. The result is a piece of silicon with a layer of aluminium oxide. Aluminium oxide is an insulator, and the resulting component is called a MOSFET (metal-oxide-semiconductor field-effect transistor).

HEck adds a wealth of electrical-engineering knowledge, explaining what needed to be replaced on the machine, what he designed, and the entire process around it.
