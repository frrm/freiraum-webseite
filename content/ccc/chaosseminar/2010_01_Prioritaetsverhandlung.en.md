In this talk, a notary and a lawyer will introduce us to copyright law in digestible portions. They will also touch on related areas of law such as trademark and patent law.

The creator of a work automatically owns the copyright to it. But that is often more theoretical: how can you prove that you, rather than someone else making the same claim, created the work? Priority depositions before a notary, who acts as a trustee, can help.
