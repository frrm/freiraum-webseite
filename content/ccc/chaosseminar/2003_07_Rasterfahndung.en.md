In September 2001, the interior ministers of the German states ordered a so-called dragnet investigation to search for alleged “sleeper” agents. On 21 September 2001, the University of Ulm also received an order to hand over student data.

What powers are the LKA (State Criminal Police Office) and other authorities given to protect internal security through dragnet investigations? How are people’s personal rights treated? How did the University of Ulm respond, and was it correct?
