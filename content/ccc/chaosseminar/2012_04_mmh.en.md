The subject of this talk is also the speaker’s master’s thesis. The thesis aims to modernise the venerable nmh as the experimental version mmh.

Mmh and nmh (New Mail Handler) are mail user agents (MUA, or mail clients) in the MH style. They consist of a collection of separate programs, each handling one task. For example, *scan* lists the messages in a folder, *show* displays a message, *comp* composes a new message, *repl* replies, and *refile* moves messages to other folders. Working with email in MH is like working with files using classic Unix tools. Experienced users can therefore use all the scripting and automation options they know from the Unix shell.

MH is for fans of the Unix philosophy who would finally like to use a conceptually elegant mail client.

This talk introduces mmh and shows step by step how to configure and customise it. It also covers personal experiences working on the project and with its community.
