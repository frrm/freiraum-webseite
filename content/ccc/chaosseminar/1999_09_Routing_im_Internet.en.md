This talk, aimed more at experienced internet users, explains how IP packets find their way through the global internet without getting lost. We will show how the worldwide network is structured and introduce and explain various routing protocols such as RIP, OSPF, and BGP. We will also introduce organisations involved in managing the internet (Internic, RIPE, and others).
