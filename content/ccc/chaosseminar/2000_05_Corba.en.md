A paradigm shift has taken place in distributed applications in recent years. Instead of communication through sockets and self-developed protocols, or classic remote procedure calls, object-oriented middleware — especially the Common Object Request Broker Architecture — is increasingly being used. This talk aims to provide an overview of CORBA and an initial look at its services, programming, and areas of application.
