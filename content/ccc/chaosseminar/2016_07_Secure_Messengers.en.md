For years we have talked about encrypted email, but to this day few people use email encryption regularly. In addition, much of our electronic communication now takes place through so-called messengers (typically on smartphones). Encryption looks very different there. Since one of the largest messengers introduced end-to-end encryption this year, ordinary users have suddenly begun communicating with one another using encryption. The debate about the security of these services has also flared up again. Many alternative messengers that were hardly used before are receiving more attention because they may be considered even more secure.

At this Chaos Seminar, we want to discuss what we expect from a communications service and what different services actually offer.
