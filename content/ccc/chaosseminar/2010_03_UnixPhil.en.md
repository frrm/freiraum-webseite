A talk about the true genius of Unix.

Unix is an impressive operating system and a first-class collection of programs. Ultimately, however, both are consequences of what is special about Unix: its philosophy. The Unix philosophy is a collection of concepts and design goals implemented in Unix.

This talk will introduce the Unix philosophy and explain why it leads to excellent software.

Beyond purely technical aspects, the Unix philosophy also offers ideas for everyday life. It is more than a set of guidelines for making good software; it is an inspiration in many ways.
