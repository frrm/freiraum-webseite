During my trip through South America, I saw a lot and had plenty of time to think. I want to share my impressions and thoughts with you. Using examples, I will explain what life is like down there, how Free Software takes shape, and how technology is used. I will mainly describe what I experienced from the perspective of someone fascinated by computers.

The talk will have a loose structure, more like a replay of a brainstorming session.
