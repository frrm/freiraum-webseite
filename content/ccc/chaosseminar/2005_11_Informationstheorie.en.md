To address the problem of transmitting information over a noisy channel, Claude E. Shannon provided a mathematical model in his 1948 paper “A Mathematical Theory of Communication”. Science only gradually came to understand its power and significance.

Today there is no doubt that Shannon founded a new field of science: his work is the foundation of all telecommunications. This includes topics such as data compression, coding, information transmission, and, last but not least, cryptography.

The talk offers a brief introduction to information theory without becoming too mathematical, and explains its key concepts and relationships in an accessible way.
