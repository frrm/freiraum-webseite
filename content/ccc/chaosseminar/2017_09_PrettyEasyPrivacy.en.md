Simple encryption for everyone: emails and messages are encrypted and anonymized "by default," without requiring much effort from users, who can keep using their usual applications. End-to-end, peer-to-peer, and without central infrastructure; open source with code reviews and support for common crypto standards (S/MIME, PGP, OTR) — and finally even routed through GNUnet for anonymization.

How is that supposed to work? Let's find out! A brief introduction to the project, the state of the software, and the current implementation of its ideas and standards, such as Trustwords as a usable extension to the concept of fingerprints, and the perennial problem of GPG keyservers.
