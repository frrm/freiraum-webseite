We originally only wanted to add two extra drives. That turned into a completely new computer and a rebuild of our server, dontpanic. The programs, techniques, and so on used for this will be briefly introduced this Monday. There will be a rough tour through all kinds of technologies, for example LVM, software RAID, backup, network boot, ramdisk, dm-crypt, and so on.

This talk gives a brief overview of the current setup, how we arrived at it, its advantages compared with the old setup, and more.
