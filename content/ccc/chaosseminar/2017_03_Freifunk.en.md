Freifunk is an initiative of independent groups working to create an open, non-commercial, decentralized network of Wi-Fi devices.

The talk covers the ideas and technology behind Freifunk. Topics include infrastructure (mesh routing with 802.11s, VPNs, gateways, IP address allocation), software (OpenWrt, batman-adv, fastd), and hardware (routers, directional wireless links). The focus will be more on the firmware.
