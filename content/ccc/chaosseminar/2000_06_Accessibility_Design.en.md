Colourful and flashy is “in” on the web. The newer the plugin, the more colourful the images, and the flashier the applets, the better the website. The “dark side of the force” is that many potential visitors cannot view these sites for various reasons. These barriers begin with huge amounts of data that nobody wants to pay to transfer, and go far beyond the fact that an internet phone rarely has a 21-inch screen built in. Yet with little effort, it is possible to ensure that new media are open to everyone, including people who are blind and want to surf the web — without giving up modern technology.
