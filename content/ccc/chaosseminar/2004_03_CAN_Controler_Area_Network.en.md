The Controller Area Network is a bus standard that is not very familiar to the average hacker. In automotive engineering, however, it is the standard of choice. The talk explains how it works and its underlying principles, and shows why CAN is so well suited to hard real-time applications. Finally, using the Philips SJA1000 as an example, it presents one way to access a CAN bus from a PC.
