The number of internet users is growing dramatically. Many newcomers, however, do not have time to familiarise themselves with the customs of this new medium. Dazzled by glossy manufacturer websites, colourful web chats, and gigabytes of downloadable software, they all too often forget:
- that behind communication partners who often act anonymously are people who may be offended by their behaviour;
- that an inbox overflowing with advertising at home is no different from the mailboxes of thousands of users whom I tell about my latest super-mega-cool homepage through some mailing list;
- that the internet can only work if everyone does their small part and behaves sensibly.

For these reasons, the internet community established rules years ago and made them available to everyone in the so-called Netiquette. Although many providers now include these rules with their application forms, we want to use this talk to explain the dos and don’ts of the internet.
