Earth at Night is a projection assembled from many individual images, showing night across the entire globe.

Across all continents, we see patterns of light that repeatedly reflect reality. Looking up at the sky is thus stripped of its romance and, except for a single natural light, handed over to society.

Afterwards, prompted by current events, we discussed the technology of nuclear power plants and tried to clarify some contradictory reports.
