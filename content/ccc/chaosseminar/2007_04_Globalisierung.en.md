Globalization is a buzzword that is on everyone's mind and accompanies us every day. But what is really behind the "specter" of globalization? Where does globalization begin, and which institutions are involved? Is there a way out of the globalization trap, and what can we as consumers do?

A guest from Attac Ulm explains what globalization means for all of us and what Attac is doing to make things better. There are probably some points of overlap with the work of the CCC, but a preliminary conversation also revealed some differences of opinion. We expect an interesting discussion.
