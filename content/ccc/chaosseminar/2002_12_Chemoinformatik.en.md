A new field of work is gradually emerging in chemistry and pharmacy. Cheminformatics means using methods from computing and programming to solve or support problems in chemistry and pharmacy. It can be divided into several areas: cheminformatics in the narrower sense, which directly supports chemists in their work, and the broad field of computational chemistry, which can be used to calculate the properties of substances without practical experiments.

This talk surveys these areas, the possibilities currently available, and the future developments that can be expected in this field.
