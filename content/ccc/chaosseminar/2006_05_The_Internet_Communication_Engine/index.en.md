The Internet Communication Engine is middleware for distributed objects. It is developed by [ZeroC](http://www.zeroc.com), which distributes it under the GPL and, when needed, under a commercial license. It has many similarities to CORBA, but aims to do some things better. This talk gives a brief introduction to distributed-object architectures and then takes a detailed look at the ICE middleware.
