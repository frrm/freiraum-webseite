Mobile ad hoc networks (MANETs for short) are a relatively new form of networking. Devices in wireless networks dynamically join together into larger groups and independently forward traffic between one another. Base stations and fixed networks thus become largely unnecessary, apart from a few points of connection. However, the dynamics of these networks greatly increase the demands on routing. This talk gives a brief overview of various wireless network technologies and explains the current state of research on MANETs.
