When people say “operating system” today, they usually mean a member of the Microsoft Windows family. Few people know that there are many alternatives, often available for a fraction of the price (or even free). They also need not fall behind their big brother from Redmond in standard software: whether a complete office suite or web browser, a game or astronomy program, high-quality applications are available for systems such as Linux, and private users can often use them free of charge. This talk aims to explain and show that Linux and the like are not just for experts and professionals, but are simply the better choice for many use cases.
