Almost no internet user thinks, while clicking through hundreds of websites each day, about all the technical processes running in the background that make it possible to search the globe for the latest information. Routing, DNS, TCP/IP, and ports are just a few of the terms that keep coming up in this context. This talk gives beginners an accessible overview of the complex interplay of components, from the click to the finished page.
