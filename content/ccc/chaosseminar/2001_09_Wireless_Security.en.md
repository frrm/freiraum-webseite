The insecurity of wireless networks has already been reported in Der Spiegel. Spies and saboteurs are now “war driving” and attacking hospitals that broadcast patient data unencrypted, according to the journalists’ nightmare scenario. Terms such as “hidden networks” and “Wired Equivalent Privacy” often create more confusion than clarity. This talk aims to explain the subject and give an overview of existing and future security mechanisms in wireless networks.
