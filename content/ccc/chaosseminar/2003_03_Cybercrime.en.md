The media repeatedly portray cybercrime as a nebulous threat to businesses, often explaining at the same time that tougher criminal laws are the only solution. But does this not distort our perception of other risks? What role do insiders play?

The talk introduces new forms of crime and describes approaches taken by the state, as well as the influence of corporate culture on security within organisations.
