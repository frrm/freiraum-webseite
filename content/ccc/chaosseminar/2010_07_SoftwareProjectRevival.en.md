A talk sharing experience and tips from my attempts to revive masqmail.

Masqmail was developed from 1999 to 2002 and was abandoned in 2005. In autumn 2008, I took it over as part of my diploma thesis. Since this spring I have been actively working on it. Taking over an old project is different from starting a new one from scratch. This talk shares lessons learned.

For preparation, my talk about masqmail may be useful, but you do not need to have seen it to follow this one: [Chaos Seminar 2009-03 on masqmail](../2009_03_masqmail)
