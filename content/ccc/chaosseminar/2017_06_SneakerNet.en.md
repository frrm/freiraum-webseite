In some places, internet access is scarce. USB sticks and hard drives can help. Data is propagated through the network (Net) on foot (sneaker). It is a bit like BitTorrent, but with much higher latency.

Marco has built a sample implementation and will demonstrate and explain it in a Q&A with Marcus.
