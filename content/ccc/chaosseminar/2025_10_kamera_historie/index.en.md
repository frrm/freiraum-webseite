# A journey through imaging technology

- We start with the first images made by humans,
- discuss chemical and electronic cameras,
- trace the development of camera technology from its beginnings
- through various 3D techniques,
- chemical imaging,
- material-identifying multispectral and hyperspectral cameras
- of the present day,
- and end with a look at the future.

This journey through imaging technology spans 30,000 years.
