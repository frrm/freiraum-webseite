### Key ghosts and teapots

While software-based attacks on sensitive data, such as Trojans and sniffers, are relatively well known, hardware-based attacks remain largely unknown both to the public and to the hacker/security community.

This talk gives an overview of possible attacks, from a tapped network cable to compromising radiation from monitors.

It also explains terms such as Keyghost and Teapot, why LEDs can be dangerous, and why Nicky Scarfo was sitting on the wrong side of the restaurant.
