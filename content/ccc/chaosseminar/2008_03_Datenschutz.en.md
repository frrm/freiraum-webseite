Data protection is omnipresent at the Chaos Computer Club. Computer security is often understood as technical data protection. Terms such as "informational self-determination" roll easily off the tongue. Everyone thinks they know all about it. But what is data protection, really? Where did it first appear, and what legal foundations exist in Germany?

This talk offers an introduction for people without a legal background and also uses examples to show where data protection problems arise.
