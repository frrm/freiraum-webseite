As we know by now, it does not always have to be Microsoft. Mac OS X (pronounced “Mac OS ten”) is a colourful mix of BSD Unix, the Mac OS interface, and a few other interesting features. The talk explains how its filesystem, processes, user management, and other operating-system tasks differ from those of its close relatives Mac OS 9, Unix, and NeXTSTEP. It also explains what a Mach kernel is.
