This talk looks at modern and future vehicles and asks how current developments in connected cars, driver assistance systems, and automated driving affect their security and might turn drivers into transparent subjects. The aim is to give a general-audience overview of the different aspects, considering car-to-car communication, in-vehicle systems, and sensors in automated vehicles as potential targets. Concrete examples from our own work and the literature illustrate the topic. At the end, we will offer a realistic assessment of the threat potential and discuss with the audience whether new in-car technologies are more a curse or a blessing.

About the speaker: Frank Kargl has been a member of the Chaos Computer Club since the early 1990s and, since 2012, has been a professor of distributed systems at the University of Ulm's Institute of Distributed Systems. His research covers a wide range of topics in computer networks, IT security, and privacy.
