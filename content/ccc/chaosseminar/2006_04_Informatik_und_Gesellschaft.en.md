2006 is the “Year of Computer Science”. Yet the public rarely sees computer science as a scientific discipline. Computer scientists are usually thought of as the people who help install or repair your Windows PC. This talk aims to clarify what computer science really is as an academic discipline, which fields it covers, what it has achieved in the past, where it touches everyday life, and what challenges it still faces. It will also start a discussion about the place of computer science in schools and society, whether that place is appropriate, and how it might be changed.
