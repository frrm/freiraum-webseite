As a short-notice replacement for the cancelled Chaos Seminar on the TKÜV, this is a short talk followed by a discussion of “biometric passports” and their security features. The talk attempts to summarise the contents of a presentation by a BSI employee at the “Conference on Security in Pervasive Computing”.
