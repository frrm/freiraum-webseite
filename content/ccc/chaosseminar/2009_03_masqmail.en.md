A talk about masqmail, the subject of Markus Schnalke's diploma thesis.

The talk is not just a description of the diploma thesis, though it does revolve around it — or rather around masqmail, which is in turn the subject of the thesis. Clear? ;-)

masqmail is a small Mail Transfer Agent (MTA) for workstations and small networks. In these settings, it can replace sendmail, qmail, postfix, and exim.

Five years ago, masqmail's author, Oliver Kurth, stopped working on it, and it has been dormant ever since. But the world of email has changed, so masqmail needs to be brought up to date. The effort is worthwhile because masqmail is unique: a small MTA that handles dial-up connections and multiple providers with ease.

The talk follows the path described in the diploma thesis, from the current, outdated masqmail to a future, modern masqmail. Comments, suggestions, and ideas from the audience will be incorporated, and there will be a discussion.

Ultimately, this talk is also intended to encourage people to use masqmail or even help develop it further.
