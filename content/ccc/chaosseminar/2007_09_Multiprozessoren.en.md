Processor manufacturers are now reaching the limits of how much they can increase processor speed. The solution is simply to connect several processors together.

Common programming languages and tools are not particularly well suited to making optimal use of modern massively parallel hardware. This talk explores the opportunities, pitfalls, and challenges involved, and presents new ideas for automating solutions to the problems that arise.
