Materials: https://ulm.ccc.de/old/chaos-seminar/computeralgebrasysteme/

This talk offers an introduction to a range of computer algebra systems such as Maple, Matlab, and Mathematica. Free systems such as MuPAD, GNU Octave, and Maxima will also be presented. Practical examples will briefly explain the syntax and use of these systems, followed by a discussion of the advantages and disadvantages of commercial and free software.
