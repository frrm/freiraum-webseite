How does voltage get into a computer?

Whether it comes from a sensor or an antenna, analogue signals from the real world are not made up of the digital 0s and 1s that computers work with. An analog-to-digital converter (ADC) connects these two worlds. This talk offers a glimpse into the fascinating world of ADCs: from basic principles and different architectures to state-of-the-art techniques in modern ADCs.
