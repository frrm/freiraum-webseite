When traceroute is used to explore network topology, the outbound and return paths probably pass through the same routers. This does not necessarily show up in their IPv6 addresses, however. To obtain topology information, the addresses belonging to routers have to be grouped together. This process is called alias resolution.

Marcus's master's thesis deals with alias resolution on a large scale (10^6).

After an introduction to IPv6 and Path MTU Discovery, the preliminary work and related work will be presented. A theoretical approach will then be developed based on the problems encountered. Finally, the implementation will be explained.
