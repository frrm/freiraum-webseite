This one is for more experienced internet travellers. The talk covers current security problems and explains fundamental mechanisms such as buffer overflows and problems with network protocols. We will also briefly discuss current virus issues. In short, it is about everything that gives system administrators headaches and hackers enjoyment. The aim is not to provide recipes for so-called script kiddies, but to explain principles and the big picture.
