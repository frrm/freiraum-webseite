The talk first covers details of the Unix-like startup process on Mac OS X.

The second part focuses on optimizing the smallest possible Mach-O executable for Mac OS X v10.6, which is one byte smaller than the smallest Mach-O executable known until then, by Amit Singh:
http://osxbook.com/blog/2009/03/15/crafting-a-tiny-mach-o-executable/.
