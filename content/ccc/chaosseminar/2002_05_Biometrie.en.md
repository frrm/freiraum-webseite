Fingerprints instead of passwords, smart cards instead of keys: this is what the future of identity verification is supposed to look like. This talk discusses identification through physical characteristics, using fingerprints as an example. It focuses on the mathematical modelling involved in processing the data, rather than on the data acquisition itself.
