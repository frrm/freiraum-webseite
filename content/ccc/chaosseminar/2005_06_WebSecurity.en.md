An increasing number of security incidents in IT systems are caused by insecure web servers or clients. Whether it is buffer overflows in software, faulty web applications, or users who are out of their depth, there is no shortage of ways to attack. The talk demonstrates vulnerabilities and possible attacks, and also discusses how users and software developers can make the World Wide Web a little safer.
