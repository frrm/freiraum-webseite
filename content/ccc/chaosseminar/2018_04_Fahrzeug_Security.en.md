Are our cars still secure?

Images of car thefts appear in the media again and again. Is it becoming harder for thieves to take over vehicles? Why does development in the automotive sector in particular show how increasing connectivity in every area of life confronts us with new questions about protection and security? This talk examines these questions from a hacker’s perspective.
