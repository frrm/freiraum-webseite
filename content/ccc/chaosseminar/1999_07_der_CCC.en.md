### Chaos in computing

The Chaos Computer Club has existed since 1981 and has had a significant influence on communications and media in Germany and internationally ever since. Terms such as the HASPA and NASA hacks are familiar even to non-specialists. For years, the CCC has also examined the effects of technology on society and individuals. Various studies on topics such as privacy and internet censorship, as well as expert opinions in bank-card trials and more, bear witness to this work. In this talk, we aim to give an impression of the CCC’s ideas, goals, and activities. We will tell stories from the past (and correct some widespread misconceptions along the way), report on current CCC projects, introduce the Ulm CCC Erfa group, and describe its activities. This talk should be particularly interesting to anyone considering active involvement with CCC Ulm.
