Mathematics offers plenty of practical things, but also — depending on your point of view — plenty of amusing and even unbelievable ones. The talk first introduces Fourier series, which featured at the Chaos Seminar a few months ago but left much more to say. As a by-product, we solve the Basel problem. This leads to the very mathematical topic of the Riemann zeta function. Finally, from the department of magic, we show that 1+2+3+4+...= -1/12.
