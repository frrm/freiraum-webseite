At the April Chaos Seminar, we will sign one another’s OpenPGP keys. A few of the organisers will first explain the procedure.

### Preparation

If you want to take part, it is useful to have already created one or more OpenPGP keys. It would also help to have familiarised yourself with the subject beforehand.

To make things a little easier for us, please send us your keyring with the user IDs to be signed at <keysigning@ulm.ccc.de> before the key signing party.

If anyone has problems, we are happy to help. In that case, however, you will have to decide for yourself how much you trust us.

You are of course welcome to come even if you do not want to sign any keys or have yours signed.

### Please bring

- A sheet of paper listing the IDs to be signed and the key fingerprint,
- a working pen,
- identity document(s) (preferably an ID card, passport, driving licence, or comparable document).
