We will get a very brief introduction to philosophy in general and then focus on the philosophy of technology. Since we do not know what background knowledge the audience has, we will keep the content flexible and draw from a selection of the usual foundational topics in the philosophy of technology (technology and/as power, the means-ends relationship, philosophy as an explanation of the world, the Chinese room, the value of technical professions and technical activity, rationalism vs. empiricism, the anthropological perspective and tool use, ethics of technology, technology as culture and sociological descriptions, technocracy, ...). Michael is an informatics student preparing his diploma thesis, with philosophy as a minor subject at the University of Ulm.
