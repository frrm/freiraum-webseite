Will we get the surveillance state we do not want, or do we need more surveillance so that anthrax pathogens will not arrive by email in future? The Telecommunications Surveillance Ordinance is the third stage of a legal framework: the constitution guarantees a fundamental right to the secrecy of mail and telecommunications, though not without exceptions; the details are governed by federal law, in this case the TKG and the G10 Act. Even more detail is set out in the associated ordinances, and, as we know, the devil is in the detail.
