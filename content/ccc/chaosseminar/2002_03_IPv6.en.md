IPv6, the next generation of the internet! Apart from the larger address space, many people know little about it. Since masquerading became popular, the address shortage has seemed less urgent and IPv6 has fallen out of the headlines. Yet IPv6 also offers other features for routing and security. The talk also provides an insight into the current state of development and explains how to connect your own computer to the IPv6 6bone test network.
