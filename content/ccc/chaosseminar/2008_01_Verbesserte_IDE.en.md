This talk is about how we will program in the future.
But the future is already here. It is called *Smalltalk*.
Tim O'Reilly once said: "The future is here; it is just not evenly distributed yet."

I will try to demonstrate a little how you write code there compared with the classic methods. The main focus will be Squeak Smalltalk, but who knows what else might come up when we talk about IDEs. Hint: Lisp. ;-)
