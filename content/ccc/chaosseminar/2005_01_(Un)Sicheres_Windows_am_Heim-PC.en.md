Personal firewall? Virus scanner? Internet security suite?

The arms race to improve defences on home PCs continues. A billion-euro market is emerging. But are these measures actually useful? Should home users not simply ask: “I just want to send email and browse the web. Do I really have to turn my PC into a fortress?”

This talk shows simple ways for non-experts to protect a home computer from many threats without installing an arsenal of security software.
