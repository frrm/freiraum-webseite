This talk introduces software that has been around for quite a few years. Many people will recognize the names, but only a few will have worked with these tools themselves — or still do.

Yet these programs and programming languages, although a little dusty, are far from obsolete. They still do their jobs faithfully, cover certain niches better than their successors, are available on many systems ... and, yes, you simply have to try them at least once! This talk is intended to encourage exactly that.

- You can experience how Unix was written.
- We will answer why you do not necessarily need Perl.
- ... and a little Unix philosophy will be hidden everywhere.

The presentation of these software treasures will be embellished with technical details and background from bygone days. Tutorials for individual tools are not on offer, but there will be plenty of ideas and starting points for your own explorations. Discussion with the audience is expressly encouraged.

Starring: ed, sed, awk
