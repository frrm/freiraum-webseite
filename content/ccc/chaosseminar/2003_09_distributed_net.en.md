Distributed computing over the internet has been familiar to a broad public since SETI@Home. The first major project of this kind, however, was distributed.net, which focuses on cryptographic and mathematical projects rather than searching for aliens. You will get a brief overview of the history and motivation behind such large projects and a look at distributed.net’s infrastructure. There will also be background information on OGR (Optimal Golomb Rulers) from a core developer.
