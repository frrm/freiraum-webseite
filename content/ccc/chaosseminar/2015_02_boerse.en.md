This talk is about how stock exchanges and other markets work. It covers different order execution strategies: a brief introduction to algorithmic trading, different order types, and how it differs from quantitative trading, among other things.

If you actually want to build a trading algorithm, you face quite different problems, such as the effect large orders have on the price itself, or how to find enough liquidity. Order routing is another important point. In particular, the approach here does not assume the efficient market hypothesis; instead it looks for market inefficiencies that can be exploited. Many of these aspects cannot be simulated using historical price data.
