The police and the domestic intelligence service eagerly collect and store data. The reality — caught between technical incompetence, surveillance mania, and political stunts — is far removed from scenarios straight out of 1984. Even so, there are plenty of reasons to keep an eye on the authorities. The talk discusses these reasons and possible ways to help yourself.
