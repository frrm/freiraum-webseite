Many people work on protecting private data. Many people argue against protecting private data. Many are tired of constantly thinking about privacy and data protection. Is privacy still relevant? Is this concept, which emerged in 1890, even desirable for us? How does privacy affect our society and its individuals? At the February 2016 Chaos Seminar, I will try to address these questions and discuss them with you.
