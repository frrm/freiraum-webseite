Dropping out of a computer science degree is almost a required qualification in hacker circles. At the same time, there are many points of contact between academic research and the hacker scene, particularly in security research. Many CCC local groups also began at universities. Drawing on his own career, Frank will explore differences and similarities, research approaches, ways of working, learning methods, and the general advantages and disadvantages of both worlds. The talk will also cover the history of CCC Ulm and how the university served as the local group’s starting point in its early days. A discussion will consider how hackers and universities can work together today and in the future.
