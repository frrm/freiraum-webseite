Which Linux distribution is best? Or should I use OpenDarwin instead? Windows is the answer! Or is it? The question of the right operating system cannot be answered objectively or universally. It is really more a matter of faith. Today the priests will have their say, after which the audience will burn them at the stake. ;-)

It proved very difficult to find enough priests, so this time the [lugu](http://www.lugulm.de) is giving us a hand. We are still looking for representatives of SuSE, Gentoo, *BSD, and other alternatives. If you care about helping others understand the advantages of your OS, just come along.

First, several systems will be introduced in short talks, followed by a discussion.
