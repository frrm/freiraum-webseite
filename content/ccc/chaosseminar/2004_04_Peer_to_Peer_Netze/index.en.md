Peer-to-peer networks have been at the center of public debate for some time. The main reason is the legal action and advertising campaigns launched by the music industry against file-sharing users in both the US and Europe. Apart from the fact that P2P does not always mean file sharing, computer scientists may find the technology behind these networks more interesting. What does "distributed system" mean, how is one organized, how do you find documents and publish your own? How can you guarantee the quality of documents while also ensuring participants' anonymity? Different P2P networks have different design goals and therefore different solutions to existing problems. This seminar presents and explains some of the most interesting ones.
