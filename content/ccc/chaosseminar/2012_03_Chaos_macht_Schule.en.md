For several years, Erfa groups have been working with educational institutions. The goal is to share our perspective on technologies such as the internet with pupils. This mainly involves visits and events at schools. The Ulm Erfa has now gained some experience with “Chaos Goes to School,” and in this Chaos Seminar we would like to tell you about it.
