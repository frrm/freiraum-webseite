A representative of the Baden-Württemberg State Criminal Police Office will provide information from the police perspective on the following topics:

- the LKA’s network investigations
- sources of danger and crime on the internet
- what to do when encountering illegal content
- the responsibilities of providers and service providers

There will be ample time for discussion afterwards. Since there is widespread uncertainty about the law and law enforcement, especially in the area of internet crime, this is an opportunity to get direct, comprehensive information and discuss our opinions and positions.
