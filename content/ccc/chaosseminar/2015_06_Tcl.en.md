This Chaos Seminar introduces the Tcl programming language. In a highly simplified nutshell, it is a mixture of C, Lisp, sh, Perl, and Lua. Tcl was once the most popular scripting language alongside Perl. Those days are over. Nevertheless, Tcl remains a language people love, and there are reasons for that. This talk will show what they are.
