PostCanvas is a small PostScript interpreter implemented in JavaScript and HTML5. Why make something like this? Well, it was an obvious fit; the components simply work together beautifully.
With a manageable amount of effort, it can interpret almost any PostScript Level 1 program, display complete EPS documents in modern web browsers without plugins, and even provide an interactive shell for small experiments.

PostCanvas is also a great way to demonstrate the origins and workings of the HTML5 Canvas element, try JavaScript as a serious programming environment, touch on the historical significance of PostScript, discuss the concept and spread of stack machines, and compare the qualities of forgiving document formats and domain-specific programming languages with strict XML data-description namespaces (e.g. SVG).

After the entertainment, the source code will be released under APLv2.
