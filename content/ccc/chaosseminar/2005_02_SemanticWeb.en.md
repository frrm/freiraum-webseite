After the “web revolution”, Berners-Lee and his colleagues at the W3C have long been working on a successor: the Semantic Web. The goal is to develop technologies for creating self-describing documents. Search engines, for example, should be able to tell that the word “Kuchen” on a page about the Swabian Alb refers to a place, while on other pages it may describe a very sugary food — and that the concept of Kuchen on a German-language page is the same as “cake” on English-language pages. Important standards such as RDF and OWL are presented in detail.
