It is high time for a Chaos Seminar on this subject! More and more tech enthusiasts are discovering our political system as a playground and are bringing ideas from the free software world into politics. Closed data management is out; open data is in!

According to Wikipedia, “open data” means the free availability and usability of [...] data. The idea is that positive developments can be set in motion when data is freely accessible to everyone.

In this talk I will explain what “positive developments” and “open data” can mean, with practical examples. Key questions include:

- What is the idea and motivation behind open data?
- Where do things stand? Which data has already been opened up?
- What have creative people of all kinds done with freely available datasets?

I will focus in particular on recent developments in Germany and Ulm. About two years ago we founded the datalove student group and the [ulmAPI.de](http://ulmAPI.de/) project. Since then we have completed several open-data projects, organised two Open Data Barcamps, and advised local and regional government offices.
