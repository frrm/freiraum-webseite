QuickTime, AVI, MPEG, DivX, and more buzzwords are thrown at us, yet hardly anyone knows how it all works. Why does my player play one format but not another? The talk sheds some light on the subject, explains what a container format and a codec are, describes the individual formats, and shows how they fit together.

Using QuickTime and/or MPEG-4, we will learn a little more about multimedia formats. I hope to take apart a .mov or .mp4 container live on stage so we can look inside together.
