Graphs are a popular model with many applications in computer science. In this talk, you will learn how a graph can be defined, how it can be represented, and what mathematical pitfalls to watch out for. Examples show that problems that look very similar sometimes have completely different solutions. You will learn how a satnav works, what Euler encountered on his walks in Königsberg, how to build the “House of Saint Nicholas,” and how computer science triumphs over mathematics.
