E-commerce — or e-business, as it is now called in the brochures of major software companies — is currently a favourite subject of management consultants, software manufacturers, and the IT press. Hardly any company fails to adorn its product with these buzzwords. A closer look at the balance sheets of Amazon & Co. makes it clear that not everything that glitters is gold. Nevertheless, shopping online is convenient and will probably find its way into many German living rooms in the future. Very few people can even begin to assess the risks and threats posed by this new kind of consumption. Users are often left with no choice but to trust providers’ promises that everything imaginable has been done to secure data and users. Using examples, we will examine the risks lurking in e-commerce, how to identify reputable providers, and what you can do to protect yourself.
