"All information should be free" was one of the CCC's first demands. In April 2002, the CCC organized a demonstration against internet censorship in Düsseldorf. Everyone thinks they know all about it. But what is censorship, really? What influence do the media have, and what special rights and duties does the press have? Where does censorship or freedom of opinion exist, in what form, and what legal foundations apply in Germany?

This talk offers an introduction for people without a legal background and uses examples to show where freedom of speech is restricted.
