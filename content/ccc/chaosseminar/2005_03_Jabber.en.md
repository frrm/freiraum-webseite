Frustrated with existing instant-messaging systems, people have been developing the open IM protocol “Jabber” since 1999. It is now an RFC standard under the name XMPP.

All kinds of Jabber applications now exist, from networked role-playing games and co-browsing to telephony signalling and email protocols. After a brief introduction for Jabber newcomers, this talk gives an overview of Jabber’s current state and possibilities.
