NetHack is a classic in computer game history. It is a turn-based, single-player Dungeons & Dragons implementation with very complex game mechanics and an equally primitive interface. Some people say the point of NetHack is to learn its game mechanics through repeated failure. We will take a shortcut through that interpretation:

- a Dungeons & Dragons crash course
- the inventory system
- nethack.alt.org

followed by trying it out in the game.

You can prepare by listening to:

- [Chaos Radio Express on NetHack](https://cre.fm/cre199-nethack)

- There was an error in the talk’s dice system explanation: only the attacker rolls; a threshold determines whether a hit lands (after applying the defender’s and attacker’s bonuses/penalties). Some implementations include a *critical hit* and a *grace*.
    Example with *d20*:
    - 1 to 14: *miss*
    - 15: *grace* (50% damage)
    - 16 to 19: *hit*
    - 20: *crit* (damage +50%)

    The original Dungeons & Dragons rules may have included only a *critical hit*.
