Zombies are an obvious and ever-present danger! To understand this problem better, we can simulate zombie epidemics and estimate how quickly a horde of zombies would overrun humanity.

Drawing on disease simulation, we will look at conventional infection models — imagine a large room in which test subjects are locked in with zombies — as well as methods for modelling an apocalypse across larger areas.

We can only wait and see whether humanity survives the evening.
