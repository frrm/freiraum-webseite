In light of current events ([data retention](https://www.vorratsdatenspeicherung.de/content/view/165/55/)), our next talk in the Chaos Seminar series is titled “Civil Disobedience Against Data Retention.”

The speakers will mainly demonstrate how to configure a computer so that it can use the distributed anonymization service [Tor](https://www.torproject.org/). The demonstration will cover several operating systems (expected to include Apple® Mac OS® X, Linux® and Microsoft® Windows®). They will also offer tips on avoiding connection data and on using Tor.

Discussion of other techniques afterward is very welcome.

The surprise guest mentioned in the original announcement unfortunately cannot attend this event, though possibly at a future talk.
