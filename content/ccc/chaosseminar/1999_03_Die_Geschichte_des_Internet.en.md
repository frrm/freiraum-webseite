Although it is already more than a quarter of a century old, the internet has only attracted broad public attention in recent years. Yet scientists and engineers have been working on this global network for more than 25 years. During that time, the number of users and connected computers has roughly doubled every year — a phenomenon unlike anything previously seen with a technology created by humans. This talk explores the history of the internet, telling stories of its beginnings, milestones, and anecdotes from the “network of networks.”
