Ubiquitous computing — the vision of ever smaller computers unobtrusively assisting people in their activities — remains a popular research topic. Following the initial enthusiasm about its opportunities and possibilities, questions about security and its effects on users’ privacy have recently become more frequent.

This talk briefly introduces the ideas behind ubiquitous computing and what has already become reality. It then highlights several privacy issues. There are many points of contact with the [previous talk](../2006_06_Personentracking), since tracking and tracing are sensitive topics here too.
